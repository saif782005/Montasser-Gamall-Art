About

The Artist

Montaser Gamal is a multidisciplinary artist based in Egypt , working in oil painting, mixed media, and digital art. His work explores how memory distorts the natural landscape, and how the natural landscape distorts memory.

He often blends abstraction with familiar forms, creating a tension between reality and imagination. His work invites viewers to question what they see and reflect on their own personal memories. Through layers of texture and shifting perspectives, his work embodies the instability of perception.
